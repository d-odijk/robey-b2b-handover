Handover to the developer

This document describes the design of the new Robey B2B dealer portal so that it can be built: what is authoritative, which values are fixed, how each component behaves, and which questions are still open. It belongs with the clickable prototype and the token files; together those three are the handover. The design history (what was different before and why it changed) lives separately in DESIGN-HISTORY.md and is not required reading.

What is authoritative

  1. The prototype: proto/prototype-responsive.html. One file, works offline (only the Inter typeface comes from Google Fonts), all twenty-one screens at every width. Open it in a browser and resize the window: this is the source for sizes, behaviour and copy. Where this document and the prototype disagree, the prototype wins.
  2. The tokens and base components: code/tokens.css, code/tokens.json, code/componenten.css and code/voorbeeld.html. Colours, sizes, radii, shadows, the size cell with all its states, buttons, labels and prices. Ready to import.
  3. This document: the rules you cannot read off a screen (behaviour, models, exceptions) and the open questions.

The design canvases with separate artboards (desktop, mobile, tablet) are background. They show older intermediate states and are no longer updated; use them only for a detail the prototype does not show.

How to use the prototype. An indicator in the bottom-right corner tells you which breakpoint you are in. Press S (or click the indicator) for a navigation bar with all screens. Everything that looks clickable works: navigation, menu, search with suggestions, filters, colour swatches on tiles and product pages, the size matrix (type quantities), the cart (quantities typed on a product page appear there immediately), the cost summary, the account pages, the gallery view on the product page (click the large photo). The prototype runs on sample data; that data is illustrative, not authoritative. Twenty-one screens: home, catalogue, four product pages, Custom Made and Outlet catalogues, cart, search results, four Downloads pages, and the account pages (orders, order detail, shipments, draft orders, draft order detail, credit notes, my details).

Reading values. The prototype is real HTML and CSS, so the browser's inspector replaces the inspect panel of a design tool: right-click, Inspect, and you see the real spacing, font sizes, colours and hover behaviour per element and per breakpoint. Prototype classes are named rb-<component>__<part> and the values refer to the tokens. The complete stylesheet is also in code/prototype-styles.css.

Glossary of the prototype class names (Dutch abbreviations): rb-tegel tile · rb-mx size matrix (__rij colour row, __cellen cells) · rb-wk cart card (__rij colour row, __cellen cells) · rb-ko cost summary · rb-pdp product page (__boven top grid, __kleur colour block, __specs specifications) · rb-galerij / rb-gal photo gallery · rb-sws / rb-sw colour swatches · rb-staal tile swatch · rb-filters / rb-fgroep / rb-frij / rb-fkleur filter column, group, row, colour · rb-fchip filter chip · rb-wagen one cart · rb-mc minicart · rb-otab / rb-okaart account table / account card · rb-zend shipment row · rb-lb gallery view (lightbox) · rb-acties action menu · rb-legen confirmation dialog.

What you adopt literally: every value (colours, spacing, radii, shadows, font sizes, hover behaviour), the structure of each component, the behaviour and the copy. What you do not adopt: the code itself. The prototype is generated design code with a single DOM and embedded images; the portal gets components with props and data from the Storefront and Customer Account APIs.

Stack and principles

  • Headless on Shopify Plus with B2B. In Shopify every colour of an article is its own product (tags color:<code> and MasterProductCode:<article number>); the portal groups those into one article with colour rows. Sizes are variants.
  • No "Add to cart" button. A quantity in a size cell is in the cart immediately (write per cell change, with a short debounce). Dealers know this from the current B2B.
  • Two carts, Stock and Preorder, checked out separately. Only Custom Made articles can be in both; catalogue articles are always Stock, outlet too. On screen the order types are called Voorraad (stock) and Voorverkoop (preorder).
  • Base prices everywhere; the dealer's additional discount is only deducted in the cost summary, as one line.
  • Gold is only for cart and checkout. Green, orange and blue are status colours (stock, backorder, preorder), red is reduced, yellow is a notice that asks for a correction. A message that is neither a status nor a correction gets no colour: the free-shipping label in the cost summary is gray.
  • Product photos come from the B2C webshop (Shopify CDN, one to three photos per colour, 1100 × 1375 on #F3F3F3). The first photo is always the product photo on the gray background; model photos follow.
  • The on-screen copy is Dutch. The portal is for Dutch dealers; the wording is part of the design and is listed under Canonical copy.

Breakpoints

Four bands, as media queries in the prototype: mobile &lt; 768, tablet portrait 768–899, tablet landscape 900–1199, desktop ≥ 1200. Two extra thresholds inside them: from 1240 the cost summary of the cart sits on one line, from 1340 the size matrix has five cells per row. Content sits in a wrap of at most 1320 px with 40 px gutters (content 1240); above a 1400 px viewport the page does not stretch further.

Typography

  • Everything else: Inter 400–900, fallback Helvetica Neue/Arial. Base 15 px, line-height:1.6, black.
  • Scale: 11 · 12 · 13 · 14 · 15 · 16 · 18 · 20 · 22 · 26 · 30 · 41.
  • Uppercase labels (ORDERTYPE, column headers): 11 px 700 letter-spacing:.07em.
  • Amounts: 800, letter-spacing:-0.005em, font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums in columns. Notation € 2.246,40 (Dutch: space after the sign, dot as thousands separator, comma as decimal separator).
  • Inputs on mobile at least 16 px, otherwise iOS Safari zooms in on focus.

Components

Black bar

One line, black: 34 px below 900, 36 px from 900, 37 px from 1200. It scrolls away with the page; at every width only the white bar is sticky. Left a check mark and "Gratis levering in Nederland vanaf € 150" (free delivery in the Netherlands from € 150). Right Downloads · Retourneren · Klantenservice · NL flag with arrow. Links white with opacity:.85, hover opacity:1 and underline. Below 900 px only the delivery line, centred; those four links then live in the menu overlay behind the hamburger. From 900 px the bar carries the links and the delivery line moves to the left, because the hamburger is gone there and the links have nowhere else to go. It needs 721 px for that, so it fits with room to spare. Whether the bar shows on a phone at all is a content choice that can be switched off later; the design does not depend on it, because the free-shipping threshold also appears in the cost summary at the moment it matters.

White bar (header)

Sticky. Logo (to home) · nav links Catalogus, Custom Made, Outlet (Outlet red) · search bar · account button · cart button. This bar runs from 900 px, with the same logo of 50, the same spacing and the same buttons as on desktop; below 900 the compact bar with the hamburger, down to 375. Only the search field moves into the bar at 1200 px; between 900 and 1199 it stays on its own row under the bar, full width, exactly as on mobile. That split is measured: with the search field in the bar the whole thing needs 1166 px (40 margin + 36 logo + 298 nav links + 262 buttons + three gaps of 56 leaves 322 for the search bar, which is exactly the point where the placeholder “Zoek op product of artikelnummer” still fits: 28 left + 226 text + 28 + 20 magnifier + 20 right). Shrinking the logo, the spacing or the placeholder to squeeze it in was rejected: the bar then stops matching the desktop artboard, and a search field that becomes an icon in one band only is the least consistent thing on the page. Without the search field the bar needs 788 px, which is why the nav links can come down to 900. Between 900 and 1199 there is no hover, because that band is tablets in landscape (1024, 1080, 1180, 1194; laptops start at 1280). There the mega menu and the account panel open on tap and close on a tap outside; the cart button already worked that way at every width.

  • Nav link 15/600, hover underlined (text-underline-offset:7px, thickness 2). Catalogus opens the mega menu.
  • Search bar #EDEDED, 999 px, 48 high, padding:0 20px 0 28px, placeholder "Zoek op product of artikelnummer", magnifier on the right. Hover #E4E4E4; focus white with box-shadow:0 0 0 2px #121212 inset. The suggestion panel opens under the width of the search bar.
  • Account button white, border gray-2, 10 px, 48 high, person icon, first name, arrow. Hover gray-1 with border gray-3. Hover opens the account panel, click goes to Orders and invoices.
  • Cart button gold, white text, same size, cart icon, (pieces), arrow. Opens the minicart on hover and click; navigates nowhere, except to the empty cart page when both carts are empty.

A button in the header opens a card under itself; the mega menu is page-wide.

Smart header. The white bar slides away (translateY(-100%), 220 ms) as soon as the user scrolls down more than 4 px and is deeper than 220 px; it comes back on 4 px upwards and always shows above 120 px. The same everywhere. While the bar is away it carries no shadow: the shadow of a bar translated out of view otherwise stays visible along the top edge of the screen. Below 1200 px only the bar with logo and buttons is sticky; the search row scrolls along and the shadow appears after scrolling.

Hover panels (mega menu, account, minicart) open on hover of their button and stay open while the mouse is on button or panel; they close 150 to 220 ms after leaving. Only the card catches the mouse, not an invisible full-width strip. Search opens on focus, closes with Escape or a click outside.

Product tile

Column with gap 9. Photo 4:5 on gray-1 (the product photo, not a model photo), label bottom-left (11/700, black "Nieuw 26/27"; Outlet red), name 15/600, article number 12 gray-6 letter-spacing:.03em, price 15/700 (13 on mobile), "Adviesprijs € …" 12 gray-6, row of colour swatches. Hover: name gold. No wishlist. One tile per article, not per colour, in the featured colour.

Price line: exactly what the product page shows. With a size surcharge the range lowest – highest (€ 12,48 – € 12,98, RRP € 24,95 – € 25,95), otherwise one figure. Reduced: red range with the old range struck through 13/500 gray-5 next to it (below on mobile). Same rule for search suggestions and sliders.

Colour swatches on the tile are a function. The swatches are those of the article, in the same order as on the product page, with the same two-colour swatches. They sit in a row: as many as fit, never more than nine, followed by a "+N" button (round, 1 px border gray-3, 11 to 12 px 600 gray-6) that expands the rest; the row then continues on a next line. Clicking a swatch switches the tile photo to that colour and marks the swatch (outline:1px solid black, outline-offset:2px); clicking the tile afterwards opens the product page with that colour selected, without jumping to the colour row. Sizes: below 768 px 24 px with 6 px gap on at most two rows (iPhone 13 mini, tile 166 px: the nine standard colours fit without "+N", with more colours eight plus "+N"); from 768 px 18 px with 6 px gap on one row (desktop, tile 312 px: nine plus "+N"; homepage slider, tile 229 px: seven plus "+N"). For the build: the swatches are buttons in front of the tile link, so the tile is a card with a link and a row of buttons, not a single <a>. In the prototype the photo switch works only for the four articles that have a product page; in the portal every colour has its own photo.

Catalogue (PLP)

Two columns: filters 220 px, results, gap 32. Heading "Catalogus (210)", on the right the view switch (grid/list) and sorting "Uitgelicht". Grid three columns, gap 34 × 26. Under the grid pagination (48 px above it): "1–12 van 210 artikelen" 13 gray-6 and 38 px buttons (Vorige disabled gray-4/gray-2, active page black, 1 2 3 4 … 18, Volgende), centred under the product column. In production 48 articles per page. On tablet and mobile a "Toon meer (N over)" button instead of pagination. On mobile the Filters button (badge with the active count) and Sorteren share the width; from 768 px they take their natural width.

Filters: at the top of the filter column the block "Toegepaste filters" with chips (gray-1, 3 px, 13 px, cross; a colour chip with a 12 px dot) and "Alles wissen", visible only when something is active; on mobile and tablet the same chips sit in a row under the Filters and Sorteren buttons, and the Filters button gets a badge with the count. Clicking a chip turns that filter off. This works in the prototype: ticking a row in a group or choosing a colour swatch creates the chip; the page opens without active filters. Groups with heading 15/600 and arrow, border-top gray-2, padding:16px 0 18px: Categorie · Kleur (swatches 24 px, directly under Categorie because colour is the second thing a dealer narrows down on) · Seizoen (Nieuw 26/27, Seizoen 25/26, Doorlopend, Outlet) · Collectie (the same nine as in the menu) · Geslacht (Heren, Dames, Junior) · Maat (buttons 13/500, border gray-3, 3 px; active black). No Merk (brand) and no Beschikbaarheid (availability): a backorder is a property of a size, not of an article. Clubs exists only in the Custom Made catalogue, as the first group. Checkbox 18 px, border gray-3, active black with white check; counts on the right 12 gray-5. A filter value with count 0 is disabled (gray-4) and the counts are recalculated after every choice, so an empty catalogue does not occur. Filter on a colour, and only the articles with that colour remain, each tile showing the photo in that colour with the swatch marked; if an article has several matching colours, the first one with stock. The count in the heading and the "Toon N artikelen" button follow; the pagination disappears while the filter is on. The filter works on colour family: a two-colour article (940 Black/Yellow) belongs to Black and to Yellow, so under Yellow the Performance 2.0 Shirt shows its Black/Yellow photo and the Grip Socks their yellow one; if an article also has the colour on its own, that one wins. In the prototype only the articles with a photo per colour remain under a colour filter (the Performance 2.0 Shirt and the Grip Socks), so the concept is visible without loading photos for every article; the filter swatches are their colours. In the portal every article has a photo per colour and it applies to everything. The other groups do not filter in the prototype. Filter panel on mobile and tablet: full screen, groups as expandable rows with a badge, checkboxes 22 px, swatches 30 px, fixed footer with Alles wissen and the black button "Toon N artikelen" that counts live.

Outlet

Its own page behind the red nav link, the same shell as the catalogue. Heading "Outlet (46)" with one line of explanation (14 gray-6, max 640 px): "Restanten en uitlopende artikelen, zolang de voorraad strekt. Alleen uit voorraad te bestellen – geen nalevering." On tablet that line sits under the heading row.

  • Label red "Outlet" bottom-left on the photo, the same place as "Nieuw 26/27". The label belongs to the article and travels to catalogue, search results, sliders and search suggestions.
  • Price red 16/700, old price struck through 13/500 gray-5 next to it (below on mobile), RRP below. The struck-through price is gray, not red.
  • Colour swatches: only the colours still in stock.
  • Filters: Korting (discount) as the first group (−60%, −50%); Seizoen and Collectie drop out; Categorie, Geslacht, Kleur and Maat stay.
  • Order type: outlet is stock by definition. No toggle, no preorder, no backorder: a size that is sold out is gone.
  • Outlet product page: like a catalogue article, with three differences. The red Outlet label sits per colour row under the colour name; the unit price is red with the old price struck through, per colour row; and per colour only the sizes still in stock are shown (no empty or orange cells, no "Beschikbaar vanaf" in the size panel). Under the prices: Outlet: zolang de voorraad strekt. Per kleur staan alleen de maten die er nog liggen; wat op is komt niet terug. In the cart the label also sits under the colour name in the colour row.
  • Price per colour: junior (116–164) is cheaper than senior. If a colour has both, the colour row shows the range; otherwise one figure. That applies to every article with a price break, not only outlet.
  • Robey's outlet photos are on white and fill the frame more; they are converted to the catalogue treatment (white to gray-1, product at 79% of the height with 10% air above, 4:5).

After Enter or via "Alle resultaten voor …". The same shell as the catalogue. Empty states

Every page without content: the same heading as the filled page, below it centred (max 560 px) a heading 20/700, one sentence 14 gray-6 and one black button (44 px) with the logical next step. No icons, no links under the button, never an empty grid or an empty table without text. Worked out: search without matches, empty cart (Naar de catalogus), no orders yet (table header stays), nothing on backorder (one line, no button). Error and loading states are not designed; proposal: yellow bar at the top with message and Retry button, the page stays.

Product page (PDP)

Availability per size

Maximum. Every editable cell knows a deliverable maximum: in stock now plus what is on its way (the same figures as in the size panel); for outlet only what is there; preorder has no maximum. If the dealer types more, the cell resets the quantity to the maximum and a yellow notice line (token notice, 13 px) appears under the cells of that colour row: "Maat 164: maximaal 60 leverbaar (huidige voorraad + wat nog binnenkomt)", without a full stop. For outlet the bracket reads "(outlet wordt niet aangevuld)". For Custom Made with preorder a middle dot and "Meer nodig? Bestel de rest via Voorverkoop" follow, a link that opens the Voorverkoop tab. The line stays until the next change in a size cell or a page change. No modal. The same in the cart.

Preorder minimum. A minimum of 15 pieces applies in Preorder per colour of an article, across all sizes of that colour. On screen it says "per product", because in Robey's coding and in Shopify each colour is its own product (RS5005-900); the article number the dealer sees (RS5005) is the MasterProductCode. So count per colour row, not per card: a card with two colours of 10 pieces each shows the notice twice and is not compliant. Three places: the explanation line under the toggle; in the colour row of the preorder matrix a yellow line while that colour is between 1 and 14 pieces ("Minimaal 15 stuks per product in Voorverkoop: nog 7 stuks nodig."); in the cart the same line per colour row. Nothing is blocked and the cost summary says nothing about it: the Afrekenen (checkout) button stays active and there is no summary notice. Sales sometimes allows an order below the minimum, on the condition that the dealer pays the full price without the additional discount, so a hard stop in the portal would be wrong. The line at the colour row is the deterrent; enforcing the rule, and applying the no-discount exception, can be added later (see Open decisions). Adding is not blocked; the limit only becomes hard at checkout. Stock has no minimum.

Calculation: additional discount on the subtotal; shipping € 5,00 while the net amount is under € 150 (Belgium and other countries: data question); VAT only in checkout. The threshold is measured on the net amount per cart: subtotal minus the dealer's additional discount, excluding shipping and excluding VAT. Confirmed by Robey on 20 September. So with a subtotal of € 199,30 and 30% discount the basis is € 139,51 and the label says € 10,49 short, while the total on the card reads € 144,51 because shipping is in it. Do not "correct" this to the total including shipping: that rule bites its own tail, because two extra pieces take the total to € 154,98 while € 5,00 shipping is still being charged, so the label would disappear before the shipping does.

Data questions for the developer

  1. Article and colour: confirm that the tags color and MasterProductCode suffice to group products into one article with colour rows, and where the order of colours comes from (the current B2B shows a fixed order per article).
  2. Swatch colour: where is the hex value per Robey colour code, and the second colour for two-colour articles (940 Black/Yellow)? Without a source, swatches stay manual work.
  3. Featured colour per article: which colour the tile shows; proposal: a metafield on the product, falling back to the first colour with stock. With an active colour filter the tile shows the filtered colour.
  4. Size surcharges: are large sizes separate variant prices? Determines the price range on tile and product page and per cell in the cart.
  5. Stock per size: show exact quantities to dealers (like "(19)") or thresholds? The design shows quantities. For the size panel and the maximum also: incoming deliveries per variant (date, quantity) and the flag "will not be replenished".
  6. Preorder: how is a production variant modelled (separate product, variant, season tag)? Determines whether the toggle works per article or per colour. Does the minimum of 15 pieces per colour also apply to Custom Made articles that only exist as Preorder, and is there an exception for reorders of a colour that was ordered before?
  7. Product copy: which article description is available (ERP, PIM, webshop), in which languages, with or without formatting?
  8. Gender and sport: do these exist as tags or metafields? Otherwise the filters cannot exist.
  9. User role: dealer or sales staff (for Wisselen), and switching between accounts through the Customer Account API (B2B company locations); is "Accountnr." the customer number from the ERP?
  10. Hide prices: a per-user setting that survives the session?
  11. Order numbers: stock orders start with B2B, preorders with VO; the ERP number is not shown. Is that right in the new setup?
  12. Shipping costs: € 150 for the Netherlands; what applies to Belgium and other countries (Shopify Markets)? The threshold itself is settled, see the cost summary.
  13. Free-shipping gap in catalogue prices: the label names the net shortfall (€ 10,49), but the dealer shops at list prices, so with 30% discount they have to add € 14,99 of product to close it. Should the label keep naming the net amount, or name what the dealer has to add at catalogue prices? The second is directly actionable but ties to no other figure in the summary, and the conversion depends on the dealer's own discount percentage. The line "Na korting" in the summary now shows the amount the threshold counts, so the figures reconcile; this question is only about which amount the label itself should name.
  14. Draft orders: Shopify draft orders or ERP? Determines whether sales can create them and whether "Aangemaakt door" can be filled.
  15. Discount agreements: does the additional discount come from the ERP as a price list per dealer or from Shopify B2B price lists? Determines how the cost summary and the Pricat download are filled.
  16. Invoices: is a backorder invoiced separately on shipment? Determines the Facturen block on the order detail.
  17. Dealer screen widths: the distribution of desktop viewport widths from the analytics of the current B2B or the webshop, to check the content width of 1320.
  18. Checkout: can the Shopify checkout mirror the cart's cost summary (additional discount as a line, shipping, VAT)?

Open decisions for Robey

  • Returns and customer service: what goes on those pages; the design is ready (Sendcloud portal, FAQ, contact form with account manager).
  • Zendingen as a separate page, or merged with the backorder information into Leveringen (expected · in transit · delivered): question for sales.
  • Discount list: does the per-EAN list still serve a purpose for dealers' purchasing systems? If so: as a download next to the Pricat, not as a page.
  • Several invoices per order: confirm that invoicing is per shipment (the order detail is designed for it).
  • Enforcing the preorder minimum: today the portal only warns at the colour row and lets the order through, because sales sometimes allows less against the full price (no additional discount). Decide later whether the portal should block below 15 pieces, or drop the discount automatically for that colour, and who may grant the exception.
  • List view of the order (one line per size) and registering a return from an order: still to be designed.
  • Outlet on mobile and tablet: the outlet catalogue exists at all sizes, the outlet product page only on desktop. Assumption: sold-out outlet sizes are never replenished.
  • Custom Made product photos: all Custom Made articles need a product photo in the same treatment as the samples (white to #F3F3F3, 4:5). Batch job.
  • Season on the tile or not; duration of the welcome strip after the first login; verify the Chamber of Commerce number on the invoice page.
